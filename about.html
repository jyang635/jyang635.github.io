About Me

Learn more about my academic journey, research interests, and professional experience in the field of Brain-Computer Interfaces.

I'm Junkai Yang, a PhD candidate in the Centre for Doctoral Training in Biomedical Artificial Intelligence (CDT BMAI) at the University of Edinburgh, where I conduct research on Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs) under the supervision of Prof. Arno Onken.

My research focuses on developing non-invasive BCI technologies to help individuals with motor disabilities regain independence through assistive devices. I specialize in signal processing techniques and machine learning algorithms for decoding neural signals.

Prior to my PhD, I completed my Master's in Bioinformatics at Georgia Institute of Technology and worked as a bioinformatician at Emory University, where I developed my expertise in scRNA-seq and interest in machine learning. Outside of research, I enjoy gaming, hiking, and staying active.
